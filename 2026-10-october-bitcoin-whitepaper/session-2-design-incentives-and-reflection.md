# Session 2 — Design, Incentives, and Reflection

**Monthly theme:** [Bitcoin Whitepaper](Introduction.md)  
**Suggested focus:** Network operation, incentives, privacy considerations, and the paper’s conclusion.

## Team activity

Revisit questions from Session 1 and share the team’s key takeaways. Discuss how the parts of the design connect, where the paper makes assumptions, and what questions remain.

## Discussion prompts

- How do blocks, timestamps, and the chain with the most proof-of-work contribute to agreement across the network?
- What role do incentives play in encouraging nodes to support the network?
- What does the paper say about privacy, and what does it not promise?
- What questions remain after reading, and what additional resource could help answer them?

## Before the session

Review your notes and bring one important insight and one open question. The chaperone shares the meeting time and venue or online link, invites members to prepare, and makes room for all voices.

## Month-end team summary

Summarize the whitepaper’s main proposal, one important design insight, and one question for continued learning. Teams may also agree on a volunteer to introduce the next month’s book.
