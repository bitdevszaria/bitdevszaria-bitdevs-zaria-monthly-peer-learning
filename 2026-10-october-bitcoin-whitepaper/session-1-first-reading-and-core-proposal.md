# Session 1 — First Reading and Core Proposal

**Monthly theme:** [Bitcoin Whitepaper](Introduction.md)  
**Suggested focus:** Problem statement, transaction model, timestamping, proof-of-work, and network sections.

## Team activity

Read and discuss the selected sections. Agree on unfamiliar terms and questions. Each participant should bring one passage or idea they found important and be ready to explain why.

## Discussion prompts

- What limitation of earlier digital-cash systems does the whitepaper aim to address?
- What does the paper mean by a peer-to-peer electronic cash system, and how does a payment move through it?
- Why is proof-of-work used, and how does it make changing the transaction history difficult?
- Which parts of the proposal are easiest to misunderstand?

## Before the session

Read the whitepaper once and note unfamiliar terms and questions. Follow the topic headings in your edition if section titles or numbering differ. The chaperone shares the meeting time and venue or online link, invites members to prepare, and makes room for all voices.
