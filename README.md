# BitDevs Zaria — Monthly Peer Learning

A community learning program for BitDevs Zaria. Each month, members work through a shared Bitcoin-focused reading in small teams, meet at least twice, and share what they learned.

## How it works

- Teams have a chaperone who coordinates the sessions and makes room for every member to contribute.
- Teams meet biweekly, with at least two sessions during the month.
- Each monthly plan suggests a sequence, but teams can adjust pacing while completing the core reading.
- Chaperones share meeting times and venues or online links directly with their teams.
- At month end, each team shares a short summary, key insight, remaining question, and next reading commitment.

## repository structure

```text
.
├── README.md
├── CONTRIBUTING.md
├── 2026-10-october-bitcoin-whitepaper/
│   ├── Introduction.md
│   ├── session-1-first-reading-and-core-proposal.md
│   └── session-2-design-incentives-and-reflection.md
└── 2026-11-november-mastering-bitcoin/
    ├── Introduction.md
    ├── session-1-foundations-and-transactions.md
    └── session-2-network-validation-and-synthesis.md
```

Create each month folder directly in the repository root using `YYYY-MM-month-topic/`. Use that folder's `Introduction.md` as the month's introduction and reading plan, then give each session its own Markdown file. Keep plans practical and clear about the required reading, session goals, discussion prompts, and monthly output. Update this README whenever a new month is added.
