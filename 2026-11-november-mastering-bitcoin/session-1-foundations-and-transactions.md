# Session 1 — Foundations and Transactions

**Monthly theme:** [Mastering Bitcoin](Introduction.md)  
**Suggested focus:** Introductory and transaction-related topics: how Bitcoin works, keys and addresses, wallets, and the structure and flow of transactions. Check corresponding topics in your edition.

## Team activity

Compare the concepts and trace the basic parts of a transaction. Note questions and any security implications to revisit.

## Discussion prompts

- In the book’s explanation, what are the main steps from creating a transaction to its confirmation?
- What is the difference between a private key, public key, address, and wallet?
- Why is a wallet better understood as a tool for managing keys than as a container holding coins?
- What does the blockchain record, and how do transactions refer to earlier outputs?

## Before the session

Confirm which edition your team is using; chapter numbering may differ. Read the selected topics and note page or chapter references in your own copy. Bring one question and one concept you can explain to a beginner. The chaperone shares the meeting time and venue or online link, invites members to prepare, and makes space for all voices.
