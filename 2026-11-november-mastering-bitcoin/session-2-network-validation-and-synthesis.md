# Session 2 — Network, Validation, and Synthesis

**Monthly theme:** [Mastering Bitcoin](Introduction.md)  
**Suggested focus:** Peer-to-peer network and blockchain topics, plus any transaction material not completed in Session 1.

## Team activity

Trace a payment from creation through network validation and confirmation. Connect the roles of keys and wallets, transaction creation, peer-to-peer relay, node validation, and blockchain confirmation.

## Discussion prompts

- How can a Bitcoin node check a transaction or block without trusting the person who sent it?
- What information is shared across the peer-to-peer network, and what can network participants infer?
- Which user security practices follow from the way keys and wallets work?
- Which book explanation, diagram, or example helped most, and what remains unclear?

## Before the session

Read the selected network and blockchain topics in your team’s edition. Bring a simple sketch of payment flow, review notes, or a glossary of key concepts. The chaperone shares the meeting time and venue or online link, invites members to prepare, and makes space for all voices.

## Month-end team output

Produce a brief concept map or verbal walkthrough connecting keys and wallets, transaction creation, peer-to-peer relay, node validation, and blockchain confirmation. Record remaining questions and choose follow-up reading as needed.
